# Decisions and open questions

## Founder requirements recovered from the shared conversations

- Physical bird traveling around a 5-inch round display; top represents better conditions, bottom worse conditions.
- Useful without an app or subscription; optional connected upgrades.
- Same-hardware goal; multi-room units with a master unit in the broader vision.
- Camera, video intercom, and baby/pet/plant monitoring remain part of that broader vision.

## Proposals, not selected production decisions

- Smaller first bench experiment: CO₂, PM2.5, temperature/humidity, bird, halo, display.
- ESP32-S3, USB-C, magnetic coupling, and display resolution.
- Draft 0.2's 0°/90°/180° demo positions, motion timings, qualification rules, and fault behavior.

## Superseded interpretations

Draft 0.1 centered on bird lean and treated the smaller scope as settled. Original-source review corrected this. Do not interpret 127 mm as a proven enclosure size or earlier body-tilt examples as perimeter coordinates.

## Open

Initial market; production scope and cost; exact sensors; actuator and reference method; orbital-to-actuator mapping; display availability; actual enclosure dimensions; classification policy; camera/audio/network architecture; identical-unit feasibility; optional services; final licensing and branding decisions.
