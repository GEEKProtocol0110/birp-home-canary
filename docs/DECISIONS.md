# Decisions and open questions

## Recovered direction

- Focus v1 on CO₂, PM2.5, temperature, humidity, bird, halo, display.
- Exclude camera, intercom, and subscriptions from v1.
- Preserve gentle expressive motion and explicit missing-reading status.

## Proposed, not validated

- Approximately 127 mm circular enclosure.
- ESP32-S3, round display, USB-C, magnetic bird mechanism.
- Control-state logic and prototype motion defaults in draft 0.1.

## Open

Exact sensors; actuator; home/reference method; motor-to-bird angle mapping; power supply; enclosure design; thresholds and timing policy; licensing; final branding and product launch scope.

Record a date, rationale, evidence, and consequences when closing a decision.
