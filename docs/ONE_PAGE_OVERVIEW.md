# BIRP — The Home Canary™

**Product concept · partner-discussion draft · October 20, 2026**

## The idea

A wall-mounted environmental monitor with a round display and a physical bird moving around its perimeter. Top represents better room conditions; bottom represents worse conditions. A halo and readable measurements provide context.

## The problem to validate

People may find a physical, understandable room-condition signal easier to notice than a phone dashboard or unexplained numbers. BIRP's first research task is to test that assumption with one defined audience.

## The intended experience

Plug in and use the core device without an app or subscription, subject to required sensor startup/readiness. Optional connected services could add remote monitoring, history, and smart-home integration. The broader vision includes camera/video intercom functions, multiple rooms and a master unit, and baby/pet/plant monitoring use cases.

## The proposed first proof

A small bench demonstration of sensor readings, cause-aware display, halo, physical perimeter movement, missing-reading status, and recovery. Exact parts, motion mechanism, enclosure, and thresholds remain unselected or unvalidated.

## Current evidence

A public concept site, an interactive browser demonstration, two original visual references, source reconciliation, and control-specification drafts. There is no working physical prototype or validated device performance in this package.

## Collaboration goal

Make the interaction understandable, choose an initial use case, and find a practical engineering/industrial-design path to the first physical proof. No partnership, license, or funding commitment is represented.
