# BIRP product vision

**BIRP — The Home Canary™**

The founder's core concept is a wall-mounted environmental display with a physical bird that travels around its perimeter. Better conditions place it toward the top; worsening conditions move it toward the bottom. The round display explains the readings while the bird and halo communicate at a glance.

The founder also selected “Better Indoor Respiratory Protection” as a working expansion of BIRP. This naming idea does not establish tested health protection, registration, or certification.

## Product requirements vs implementation status

| Smoke and carbon-monoxide integration | Requested long-term exploration; not present in the current prototype scope or a supported safety claim |

## Proposed first experiment

A smaller sensor-to-indicator bench experiment can test the central interaction with CO₂, PM2.5, temperature/humidity, the bird, halo, and display. It does not redefine the final product or prove the camera/intercom roadmap. The founder has not selected final production hardware.

## Independent coordinates

- **Orbital position:** where the bird sits around the display, measured from top.
- **Body tilt:** how the bird leans at that position.

Validate orbital movement first. Later body gestures must preserve the main indicator's meaning. The website demonstrates selected states, not actual sensing, calibrated air thresholds, or a validated mechanism.
