## How to use them

The logo provides the original white-bird, warm-yellow, and halo identity. Use it as a reference for brand continuity; the illustration does not establish a registered mark.

The exploded view is original project concept art, not a buildable engineering drawing, CAD model, validated bill of materials, or certification record. It includes optional modules and illustrative labels. Bird placement and layer details vary across the artwork; the founder's perimeter-motion requirement in PRODUCT_VISION remains authoritative.

Do not infer component fit, display availability, sensor selection, power budget, actuator geometry, or smoke/CO functionality from the illustration. Preserve the original art and label it as a concept when published.
