01 / THE IDEA

Less decoding.
More understanding.

A glance across the room should tell you something.

The BIRP concept brings three signals together: a physical bird that travels around the display, a halo that changes color, and a display that explains the measurement behind the change.

The bird is the personality. The readings are the reason. When a reading is missing, BIRP should say so clearly.

02 / THE FIRST BENCH EXPERIMENT

A focused start.
A living little object.

Four measurements. One expressive idea.
The proposed bench build tests the sensing-to-motion loop. The broader vision includes optional connected services, camera/video intercom, and room-to-room monitoring.

01 CO₂
Carbon-dioxide readings with a visible explanation when the configured tier changes.
02 PM2.5
Fine-particle readings, evaluated separately so one measurement cannot hide the other.
03 Temperature & humidity
Room measurements shown alongside the air-quality channels.
04 Bird, halo & display
Perimeter movement and a readable reason for each state. Core standalone use is intended to work without an app or subscription.
